Description

A burger is a popular sandwich made with a cooked beef patty, a soft bun, vegetables, and other toppings.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Ground beef
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Shape the ground beef into a patty.
  2. Cook the patty until it is fully cooked.
  3. Slice the vegetables.
  4. Place the patty inside the bun.
  5. Add cheese and vegetables.
  6. Close the burger and serve.
