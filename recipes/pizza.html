Description

Pizza is a popular dish made with a baked dough base, tomato sauce, cheese, and different toppings.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Bell pepper

Steps

  1. Prepare the pizza dough.
  2. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  3. Add cheese and your toppings.
  4. Place the pizza in a preheated oven.
  5. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese melts.
  6. Remove from the oven and serve.
