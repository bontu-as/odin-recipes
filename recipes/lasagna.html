Description

Lasagna is a delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, sauce, cheese, and meat.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese
  • Onion

Steps

  1. Prepare the sauce.
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles.
  3. Layer the noodles, sauce, and cheese.
  4. Bake until fully cooked.
  5. Let it cool and serve.
